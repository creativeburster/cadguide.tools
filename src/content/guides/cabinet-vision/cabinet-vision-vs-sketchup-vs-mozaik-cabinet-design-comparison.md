# Cabinet Vision vs SketchUp vs Mozaik: Cabinet Design Software Comparison for Woodworkers

I've worked with all three of these cabinet design tools in different shop environments. Cabinet Vision, SketchUp, and Mozaik represent three very different approaches to cabinet design — full parametric manufacturing, general 3D modeling, and affordable CNC-ready design. Understanding their differences helps woodworkers and cabinet shops choose the right tool for their specific needs and budget.

## Quick Comparison

| Feature | Cabinet Vision | SketchUp | Mozaik |
|---------|---------------|----------|--------|
| Type | Parametric cabinet CAD | General 3D modeler | Cabinet-specific CAD |
| Parametric assemblies | Yes | No | Yes (limited) |
| Cut list generation | Yes (automatic) | No (manual or plugin) | Yes (automatic) |

| Target user | Production shops | Designers/hobbyists | Small-to-mid shops |

## Cabinet Vision

### Strengths

- **Full parametric system**: Change one parameter, all parts update automatically
- **Complete S2M workflow**: Design to CNC machine code in one system
- **Construction methods**: Both frameless and face frame with detailed joinery options
- **Extensive hardware libraries**: Hinges, slides, knobs from major manufacturers
- **Material libraries**: Comprehensive board, edge banding, and finish options
- **CNC integration**: Direct connection to all major CNC brands
- **Nesting**: Built-in sheet optimization for CNC routing
- **Production reports**: Cut lists, part labels, assembly drawings, shop drawings
- **Multi-room projects**: Handle entire houses with multiple rooms
- **Commercial casework**: Suitable for large commercial projects

### Weaknesses

- **High cost**: Most expensive of the three, especially the Premium level
- **Steep learning curve**: Can take weeks to become proficient
- **Overkill for simple projects**: Too complex for one-off custom pieces
- **No Mac version**: Windows only
- **Annual maintenance fees**: Ongoing cost for updates and support

### Best For

- **Production cabinet shops** with CNC equipment
- **Commercial casework** manufacturers
- **Shops** that need complete design-to-manufacturing integration
- **Large-scale operations** with multiple CNC machines
- **Shops** that need detailed cut lists and assembly drawings

## SketchUp

### Strengths

- **Easiest to learn**: Intuitive interface, most users productive in hours
- **Excellent 3D modeling**: Best free-form 3D modeling of the three
- **Low cost**: Pro version is $300/year, free version available
- **Large user community**: Extensive tutorials, extensions, and 3D warehouse
- **Flexible**: Not limited to cabinets — design anything
- **Client presentations**: Excellent 3D visuals for client communication
- **Cross-platform**: Windows and Mac
- **Extensions**: Cabinet-specific extensions available (e.g., CabMaker, SketchList)

### Weaknesses

- **Not parametric**: No automatic part updates when dimensions change
- **No built-in cut list**: Requires manual calculation or third-party plugins
- **No CNC output**: Requires third-party plugins for G-code generation
- **No construction methods**: Doesn't understand cabinet joinery
- **No hardware libraries**: No built-in hinge or slide placement
- **Manual everything**: Each part must be drawn individually
- **Not production-ready**: Design only — no manufacturing output

### Best For

- **Designers** who need client presentation visuals
- **Hobbyists** and small custom shops
- **One-off custom furniture** where parametric rules don't apply
- **Shops** that cut manually (no CNC)
- **Users** who need a general 3D modeler, not just cabinet software
- **Budget-conscious** users who can't afford dedicated cabinet software

## Mozaik

### Strengths

- **Cabinet-specific**: Designed specifically for cabinets, not a general modeler
- **Affordable CNC integration**: CNC output at a lower price than Cabinet Vision
- **Parametric cabinets**: Change dimensions, parts update automatically
- **Cut list included**: Automatic cut list generation
- **Nesting**: Built-in sheet optimization
- **CNC output**: Direct G-code generation for popular CNC machines
- **Faster to learn** than Cabinet Vision
- **Subscription pricing**: Lower upfront cost
- **Good for small shops**: Right-sized for small-to-medium operations
- **Quick quotes**: Estimate material costs and pricing quickly

### Weaknesses

- **Less powerful** than Cabinet Vision for complex projects
- **Limited commercial casework**: Not as strong for large commercial jobs
- **Fewer hardware options**: Smaller hardware library
- **Less customization**: Fewer options for non-standard construction
- **Basic 3D visualization**: Not as polished as SketchUp or Cabinet Vision
- **Windows only**: No Mac version
- **Subscription model**: Ongoing annual cost

### Best For

- **Small-to-medium cabinet shops** with CNC equipment
- **Shops** transitioning from manual to CNC production
- **Budget-conscious** shops that need CNC output
- **Residential cabinet** projects (kitchens, baths, closets)
- **Shops** that want parametric cabinets without Cabinet Vision's complexity

## Feature-by-Feature Comparison

### Cabinet Design

- **Cabinet Vision**: Most comprehensive. Parametric assemblies with automatic part generation. Both frameless and face frame. Detailed joinery options. Hardware libraries.
- **SketchUp**: Manual 3D modeling. Each part drawn individually. No cabinet-specific intelligence. Good for custom shapes but slow for standard cabinets.
- **Mozaik**: Parametric cabinets with automatic part generation. Both frameless and face frame. Good for standard cabinets but less flexible for custom designs.

### Cut List and Production

- **Cabinet Vision**: Automatic cut list, part labels, assembly drawings, shop drawings. Complete production documentation.
- **SketchUp**: No built-in cut list. Third-party plugins (e.g., CutList Plus) can generate cut lists from SketchUp models, but it's a separate workflow.
- **Mozaik**: Automatic cut list and part labels. Basic assembly drawings. Good for small shop production.

### CNC Integration

- **Cabinet Vision**: Full S2M with direct machine connection. Supports all major CNC brands. Nesting, toolpaths, drilling, grooving, routing. Edge bander integration.
- **SketchUp**: No CNC output. Third-party plugins (e.g., SketchUCam, Phlatscript) can generate basic G-code, but not production-grade.
- **Mozaik**: Integrated CNC output. Supports popular CNC brands. Nesting and basic toolpaths. Good for shops with one CNC machine.

### 3D Visualization

- **Cabinet Vision**: Good 3D with material application and rendering. Professional but not as polished as SketchUp for presentations.
- **SketchUp**: Best 3D visualization. Excellent for client presentations. Large component library (3D Warehouse). Photorealistic rendering with plugins (V-Ray, Enscape).
- **Mozaik**: Basic 3D visualization. Adequate for design review but not for high-end client presentations.

### Ease of Use

- **Cabinet Vision**: Steepest learning curve. Weeks to become proficient. Training courses recommended.
- **SketchUp**: Easiest to learn. Most users productive in hours. Extensive free tutorials.
- **Mozaik**: Moderate learning curve. Days to become proficient. Online training available.

## Which Should You Choose?

### Choose Cabinet Vision If:
- You're a **production cabinet shop** with CNC equipment
- You need **complete design-to-manufacturing** integration
- You do **commercial casework** or large residential projects
- You need **detailed production reports** (cut lists, labels, assembly drawings)
- You have multiple **CNC machines** from different manufacturers
- Budget is not the primary concern

### Choose SketchUp If:
- You're a **designer** who needs client presentation visuals
- You're a **hobbyist** or small custom shop
- You cut **manually** (no CNC)
- You need a **general 3D modeler**, not just cabinet software
- **Budget** is a primary concern
- You're on **Mac**

### Choose Mozaik If:
- You're a **small-to-medium cabinet shop** with CNC
- You want **parametric cabinets** without Cabinet Vision's complexity
- You need **CNC output** at an affordable price
- You do primarily **residential cabinets** (kitchens, baths, closets)
- You're **transitioning from manual to CNC** production
- You want a **subscription** rather than a large upfront cost

## Summary

Cabinet Vision, SketchUp, and Mozaik serve different segments of the cabinet design market. Cabinet Vision is the production powerhouse — full parametric design, complete S2M workflow, and comprehensive CNC integration, ideal for production shops and commercial casework but at the highest cost and steepest learning curve. SketchUp is the versatile 3D modeler — easiest to learn, best for client presentations, and most affordable, but lacks cabinet-specific intelligence and production output. Mozaik is the middle ground — cabinet-specific with parametric design and CNC output at an affordable subscription price, ideal for small-to-medium shops with CNC equipment. For production shops with CNC, Cabinet Vision is the most capable. For designers and hobbyists, SketchUp is the most accessible. For small shops needing CNC output without Cabinet Vision's complexity, Mozaik is the best value.
