# Alibre Design Perpetual License: Understanding Maintenance, Upgrades, and Ownership

Alibre Design is one of the few mid-range 3D CAD tools that still offers perpetual licenses. I've been using it since 2023 and have gone through two upgrade cycles. Here's exactly how the licensing works — because the marketing page doesn't explain the details clearly.

## What You Actually Own

When you purchase Alibre Design Professional or Expert, you receive:

- **A perpetual license** — This never expires. You can use the current version forever, even if you never pay another cent.
- **A serial number** — Tied to you (not a dongle). You can install on up to 2 machines (1 active at a time).
- **1 year of maintenance** — Included with the initial purchase. Covers updates, upgrades, and technical support.

## What Maintenance Includes

The annual maintenance subscription (currently ~$395/year for Professional, ~$595/year for Expert) covers:

- **All version updates** — Minor point releases (2026.1, 2026.2, etc.)
- **Major version upgrades** — When Alibre releases 2027, you get it free if your maintenance is active
- **Technical support** — Email and forum support from Alibre's team
- **Cloud storage** — 5 GB of Alibre Cloud storage for file sharing
- **Beta access** — Early access to new features before public release

## What Happens When You Stop Paying Maintenance

This is the key question. If you let maintenance lapse:

1. **You keep your current version** — The software continues to work. No feature lockout, no watermarks, no reduced functionality.
2. **You stop receiving updates** — No new versions, no bug fixes, no feature additions.
3. **You lose technical support** — No email or phone support. Forum access remains (free community support).
4. **You lose cloud storage** — Your Alibre Cloud account is deactivated. Download your files before lapse.

This is fundamentally different from Autodesk's subscription model, where stopping payment means the software stops working entirely.

## Reinstating Lapsed Maintenance

If you let maintenance lapse and later want to reinstate:

- **Within 12 months of lapse**: Pay the annual maintenance fee plus a $100 reinstatement fee. You get the current version immediately.
- **After 12 months of lapse**: You must purchase a new license at the current price. There is no "catch-up" maintenance option.

**Recommendation**: If you're unsure about continuing, keep maintenance active for at least the first 2 years. The bug fixes and feature improvements in the first 2 years of a version cycle are significant. After that, evaluate whether the updates justify the cost.

## License Transfer

You can transfer your Alibre license to another person or company:

1. Contact Alibre support with your serial number and the new owner's information.
2. Alibre updates the registration in their database.
3. The new owner downloads and installs the software.
4. The transfer fee is $100 (one-time).

This is a significant advantage over subscription-based CAD (SolidWorks, Fusion 360) where you can't transfer or resell your license.

## Network/Floating Licensing

Alibre does **not** offer network floating licenses. Each license is locked to a named user and allows installation on 2 machines (e.g., desktop + laptop) with 1 active session at a time.

For teams that need floating licenses, the workaround is:
1. Purchase individual licenses for each user.
2. Use a license server script that manages which user is "active" — but this is manual and not officially supported.

If floating licensing is a hard requirement, Alibre is not the right choice. Look at IronCAD or BricsCAD for perpetual + floating options.
