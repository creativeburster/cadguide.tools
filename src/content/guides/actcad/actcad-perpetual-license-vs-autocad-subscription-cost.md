# ActCAD Perpetual License vs AutoCAD Subscription: Cost Comparison and Break-Even Analysis

We ran the numbers for our 25-seat office when considering ActCAD as an AutoCAD replacement. The headline savings are significant — ActCAD perpetual costs roughly the same as one year of AutoCAD subscription. But the full picture has nuances. Here's the breakdown.

## Upfront Cost Comparison (Per Seat)

| Item | AutoCAD Subscription | ActCAD Professional |
|------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Year 1 license | $2,030/year | $499 one-time |
| Year 2 | $2,030/year | $0 (owned) |
| Year 3 | $2,030/year | $0 (owned) |
| **3-year total** | **$6,090** | **$499** |
| Upgrade (Year 3) | N/A (always current) | $299 (optional) |
| **3-year total w/ upgrade** | **$6,090** | **$798** |

The savings per seat over 3 years: **$5,292**. For 25 seats: **$132,300**.

## What You Get With Each Option

**AutoCAD subscription includes:**
- All version updates and new releases
- AutoCAD web and mobile apps
- Autodesk Drive cloud storage (25 GB)
- Autodesk App Store access
- Specialized toolsets (Architecture, Mechanical, Electrical, Map 3D, Plant 3D, Raster Design)

**ActCAD Professional perpetual includes:**
- Permanent license — no expiry, no recurring fees
- One year of free updates and technical support
- ActCAD LISP engine (AutoLISP compatible)
- ActCAD Cloud (viewing and light editing via browser)
- Built-in PDF to DWG converter
- Raster image to vector conversion

## The Hidden Costs of ActCAD

The headline price doesn't tell the full story. Factor in:

1. **Annual Support and Updates (SSP)** — After year 1, optional Software Support Plan costs ~$199/year/seat. Without it, you keep using the current version but get no updates or tech support.

2. **Training and migration** — Plan for 2-3 days of productivity loss per user. At $50/hour loaded labor cost, that's ~$1,200/seat in hidden migration cost.

3. **IT deployment time** — Deploying ActCAD across a network is simpler than AutoCAD (no FlexLM server), but still requires 1-2 hours of IT time per seat for installation and configuration.

4. **Missing specialized toolsets** — If your team uses AutoCAD Architecture or Mechanical, ActCAD doesn't have direct equivalents. You'd need to replicate workflows with LISP or third-party plugins.

## The Hidden Costs of AutoCAD Subscription

1. **Price increases** — Autodesk has raised subscription prices annually by 5-10%. Budget for $2,200+/year by year 3.

2. **Cloud dependency** — Subscription requires periodic online verification. If Autodesk's license server is down, users can't work (happened twice in 2025).

3. **Forced upgrades** — Subscription includes updates, but Autodesk sometimes discontinues features or changes APIs, forcing LISP and plugin rewrites.

4. **Single-user lock** — AutoCAD subscriptions are single-user. No floating without an additional FlexLM server and network license type (which costs more).

## Break-Even Analysis

For a 10-seat office:

| Timeline | AutoCAD Cost | ActCAD Cost | Savings |
|----------|-------------|-------------|---------|
| Year 1 | $20,300 | $4,990 + $12,000 (training) = $16,990 | $3,310 |
| Year 2 | $20,300 | $0 | $20,300 |
| Year 3 | $22,330 (price increase) | $1,990 (SSP) | $20,340 |
| **3-year total** | **$62,930** | **$18,980** | **$43,950** |

The break-even point is **Month 8** — after that, ActCAD's perpetual model generates pure savings.

## When ActCAD Doesn't Make Sense

- Your team relies heavily on AutoCAD vertical toolsets (Architecture, Civil 3D, Plant 3D)
- You need Autodesk Forge API integration
- You collaborate with partners who require live DWG roundtrip with zero compatibility risk
- You use AutoCAD's Sheet Set Manager extensively (ActCAD has no equivalent)

## When ActCAD Clearly Wins

- Pure 2D drafting workflows
- Small to mid-size firms with tight budgets
- Organizations that don't need annual version updates
- Firms that have already standardized on generic DWG workflows without vertical-specific objects
