# Shapr3D Subscription Pricing: Is the Pro Plan Worth It for Professional Use?

Shapr3D moved to subscription-only pricing in 2023. There's no perpetual license option. As someone who pays for the Pro plan out of pocket, here's an honest assessment of whether it's worth the money.

## Plan Comparison

| Feature | Free | Pro | Business |
|---------|------|-----|----------|
| Price | $0 | $299/year ($24.92/mo) | $499/year ($41.58/mo) |
| Models | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Documents | 2 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Export formats | STL, 3MF, OBJ | + STEP, IGES, DWG, DXF, X_T | + All formats |
| 2D drawings | No | Yes | Yes |
| Cloud sync | No | Yes | Yes |
| Commercial use | No | Yes | Yes |
| Team workspace | No | No | Yes |
| Admin controls | No | No | Yes |
| SSO | No | No | Yes |
| Priority support | No | No | Yes |

## The Free Plan: What Can You Actually Do?

The free plan is surprisingly capable for non-commercial use:

- Full 3D modeling tools (extrude, revolve, sweep, loft, fillet, shell, pattern)
- All sketch tools and constraints
- STL and 3MF export (for 3D printing)
- Unlimited models (but only 2 documents — a document can contain multiple bodies)

**Limitations that matter for professional use:**
- **No STEP export** — You can't send parts to CNC machine shops. This is the #1 reason to upgrade.
- **No 2D drawings** — You can't produce manufacturing drawings.
- **No commercial use** — The license explicitly prohibits using free models for paid work.
- **No cloud sync** — Files stay on the iPad only. No desktop access.

**Verdict**: The free plan is perfect for learning, hobby projects, and 3D printing at home. For any professional work, it's insufficient.

## The Pro Plan: What You Get

The Pro plan ($299/year) unlocks everything a professional product designer needs:

### STEP Export — The Critical Feature

STEP export is the single most important Pro feature. Without it, you can't:
- Send parts to CNC machine shops (they need STEP for CAM)
- Share models with SolidWorks, Fusion 360, or Inventor users
- Submit parts to injection molding services
- Participate in professional CAD file exchange

If you need STEP export, you need Pro. There's no workaround.

### 2D Drawings

The Pro plan includes 2D drawing generation:
- Orthographic views (front, top, right)
- Isometric views
- Section views
- Detail views
- Dimensioning (ANSI and ISO)
- Title block customization
- PDF and DWG export

The drawing module is basic compared to SolidWorks or AutoCAD, but sufficient for simple manufacturing drawings.

### Cloud Sync

Files sync between iPad, Mac, and Windows desktop. This is essential if you:
- Start a design on iPad (concept phase)
- Continue on desktop (detailing, CAM)
- Need to access files from multiple devices

### Commercial License

The Pro plan explicitly permits commercial use. You can design products for paying clients without license concerns.

## Is Pro Worth $299/year?

### For Professional Product Designers: Yes

$299/year is **$24.92/month** — less than a Netflix subscription. For a tool that generates revenue, this is trivially cheap. Compare:

| CAD Tool | Annual Cost |
|----------|------------|
| Shapr3D Pro | $299 |
| Fusion 360 (Personal) | $0 (limited) |
| Fusion 360 (Commercial) | $545 |
| SolidWorks Standard | $2,995+ |
| AutoCAD | $2,030+ |

Shapr3D Pro is the cheapest commercial 3D CAD with STEP export. The iPad mobility is a bonus that no competitor offers.

### For Hobbyists and Students: Maybe Not

If you only 3D print your designs and don't need commercial rights, the free plan is sufficient. STEP export isn't needed if you're printing on your own printer — STL works fine.

Students should check if their school provides Shapr3D Education licenses (free for accredited institutions).

### For Teams: Consider Business Plan

The Business plan ($499/year) adds:
- **Team workspaces** — Shared file space for collaborative design
- **Admin controls** — Manage licenses and access across the team
- **SSO** — Single sign-on for enterprise IT

For a 1-2 person team, Pro is sufficient. For 5+ designers, the Business plan's team workspace and admin controls justify the extra $200/year.

## The Downside of Subscription-Only

The biggest complaint about Shapr3D is the lack of a perpetual license. If you stop paying:
- You lose access to all Pro features
- You can still view and export STL (free plan)
- But you can't export STEP or create drawings
- Your cloud-synced files remain accessible but can't be edited with Pro tools

This is the same model as Adobe Creative Cloud and Autodesk products. You're renting, not owning. If perpetual licensing is important to you, look at Alibre Design or IronCAD instead.

## Hidden Costs

- **iPad hardware**: Shapr3D requires an iPad Pro (M1 or later recommended) + Apple Pencil 2. If you don't already own these, factor in $1,100–$2,400.
- **Desktop Shapr3D**: Included with Pro subscription (no extra cost). Runs on Mac and Windows.
- **Internet for cloud sync**: Not a direct cost, but cloud sync requires an internet connection. Offline mode works but syncs when reconnected.

## Our Recommendation

If you're a professional product designer who values mobility, Shapr3D Pro at $299/year is excellent value. The STEP export alone justifies the cost. The iPad workflow is genuinely faster for concept design than any desktop CAD.

If you're a hobbyist, stick with the free plan until you need STEP export or commercial rights. The upgrade cost is low enough that you can wait until you have a paying project.
